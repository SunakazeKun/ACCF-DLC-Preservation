# Animal Crossing: City Folk DLC Preservation
**Animal Crossing: City Folk** (known as **Let's Go to the City** in Europe and Australia) featured a lot of post-launch
extra content released via WiiConnect24. These consist of 91 new items and 10 exclusive designs. The player would
receive distributed items in special letters that were hand-delivered by Pete. Designs were obtained from Wendell. This
project aims to preserve all the *Animal Crossing: City Folk* DLC content. However, since the distribution data was only
shared in very small timeframes, getting hold of authentic data is very difficult and requires many save files to be
analyzed. Thanks to community efforts, all items (including three unreleased ones), most designs and many of the letters
have already been preserved. That said, there's still a lot left to preserve.

**If you played the game between 2008-2013, there's a good chance that your save data count contain DLC-related data.
You can contribute by sharing your save data or, if you wish to keep your save private or are unable to dump your save,
by providing screenshots. Below you can find an overview of information we are still missing as well as methods to dump
your save data. Feel free to reach out on my Discord server (https://discord.gg/C3px4rejUX) or via e-mail
([aurumsmods@gmail.com](mailto:aurumsmods@gmail.com)). With a few steps, you can easily contribute to this
preservation effort. Huge thanks in advance!**

## How you can help
Luckily, to contribute to this project, there's not much you'd have to do. **All that I need are the extracted
``rvforest.dat``and ``wc24dl.vff`` files from the game's save data**. On a homebrew-enabled Wii, you can use apps like
*SaveGame Manager GX* to dump your save data. If you don't want to mod your console, you'll have to make a backup of
your console's NAND. **In case you need help performing these steps or to send me save data, feel free to contact me
([Discord](https://discord.gg/C3px4rejUX), [e-mail](mailto:aurumsmods@gmail.com))!**

### Requirements
- An SD card formatted to FAT32 with at least 600 MB free space.
- A way of accessing the SD card on your computer.
- A Wii console on version 3.0 or newer.

### With console modding
For this, you'll have to install homebrew on your Wii console. This process is fairly simple and very safe. Breaking
your console is practically impossible if you clearly follow guides on setting up homebrew. I generally recommend the
[Wii Hacks Guide](https://wii.hacks.guide/) for modding your Wii system. You don't have to follow the entire guide,
it's fine to do everything up to the installation of the Open Shop Channel. After that, you'll have to download
*SaveGame Manager GX* ([WiiBrew page](https://wiibrew.org/wiki/SaveGame_Manager_GX), 
[Open Shop Channel](https://oscwii.org/library/app/SaveGame_Manager_GX)). Then, you launch the app via the Homebrew
Channel to extract save data to your SD card. After that, there should be two files called ``rvforest.dat`` and
``wc24dl.vff`` on your SD card. These are the files necessary for further analysis of DLC content.

### Without console modding
That's okay. In this case, you can create a backup of your Wii's NAND and load it into Dolphin Emulator to access the
save data. This process will leave no traces on your console. However, I recommend that we work this out together, so
feel free to contact me [on my Discord server](https://discord.gg/C3px4rejUX).
